Welcome to My Personal Website

Most people default into careers. I did too. Now I'm testing whether I should have explored first.

At 18, I chose pharmacy because it felt like the "right" credential after medical school rejection. For 15 years, I stayed—good income, respectable career, but something was missing. I never asked: does this actually suit me? Or are there other work contexts where my strengths shine brighter?

This site is what I wish younger me had done before deciding anything: systematic exploration. I'm testing four working identities—pharmacy, law, cybersecurity, and actuary—through real action, not abstract planning. Each identity has an experiment plan (aim, hypothesis, methods) and a live feed showing progress, results, and adjustments.

My current CV lives on LinkedIn. Longer writing goes on Substack. Here, you see the actual work: what I'm building, learning, and deciding whether to pursue further. This is evidence-based career testing. No credentials chasing. Just deliberate exploration.
